Break the arm. It keeps writing.

This robot arm has never seen the damage you are about to do, and nothing retrains it. It tries a few moves, remembers what each one did, and carries on.

By Chris Martinez · Code on GitHub

Break it

Every change starts the word again. The agents practiced on rewired arms with motors from 0.5× to 1.5× strength and no drag. Past that, they are on their own.

Who is driving

A piece of a letter is inked when the pen touches it. The thin gray line is where the pen really went.

Past what they practiced on, ours still inks 75 to 95% of the word and the recurrent agent 35 to 75%. MAML, which adapts by changing its weights instead of by memory, never learned the arm. Ours worked out by itself what an engineer would hand-write for this arm, the self-calibrating controller above. That controller is ahead on plain strength changes, and ours is ahead on drag and when damage combines.
